Why Visit Kashmir

Kashmir's surreal landscapes feel like stepping into an ancient painting. Experience profound tranquility gliding across mirrored lakes in a magnificent wooden houseboat.

  • World-class skiing down the slopes of Gulmarg
  • Vibrant Mughal gardens blooming with exotic flowers
  • Exclusive artisanal cashmere and intricate saffron trades
  • Trekking trails through remote alpine meadows
